IX. FAQs


       HL:
       Frequently Asked Questions


       What is The Jammy Egg?
             ➔​ The Jammy Egg is a soft-boiled egg. It has a rich, golden yolk. It comes fully cooked,
                 peeled, and ready to eat. Jammy is made from real, natural family-farmed eggs from
                 Chino Valley Ranchers.


       Is the egg fully cooked?
             ➔​ Yes. The Jammy Egg is fully cooked and ready to enjoy. No boiling, peeling, or timing
                 required.


       How is Jammy different from a hard-boiled egg?


                                                                                                            3
              ➔​ A hard-boiled egg has a fully firm yolk. The Jammy Egg has a softer, creamier center,
                  similar to a 6-minute soft-boiled egg. The soft, jammy texture of the yolk is a key
                  difference.


        How is The Jammy Egg packaged and stored?
              ➔​ Each bag includes four individually wrapped and sealed Jammy Eggs. Keep the bag
                  refrigerated and enjoy by the date printed on the package.


        How do I use The Jammy Egg?
              ➔​ You can eat it on its own with salt, pepper, or your favorite toppings. Or add it to foods
                  like toast, ramen, salads, sandwiches, grain bowls, and more.


        Do I eat it hot or cold?
              ➔​ The Jammy Egg is ready to eat cold, straight from the fridge. It can also be eaten
                  warm. Just add it to boiling water for 30 seconds.


        How are Jammy Eggs raised and produced?
              ➔​ Jammy Eggs come from cage-free hens raised with care. Our farms are American
                  Humane Certified™, following rigorous standards designed to support the health, safety,
                  and well-being of our hens. We also use a premium feed recipe developed by Chino
                  Valley Ranchers, helping create the deep golden yolk color Jammy is known for.
